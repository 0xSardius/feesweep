## Style direction — Neobrutalism

The entire UI follows neobrutalism, in service of shareable, meme-native surfaces:

- **Borders:** thick and solid — `border-2` (controls) to `border-[3px]` (cards, sections), always in `border-foreground` (not `border-border`, which is for hairline dividers only).
- **Shadows:** hard offsets, no blur — use `--shadow-brutal` (4px 4px 0) and `--shadow-brutal-lg` (8px 8px 0), both defined in `globals.css` and colored by `--foreground`.
- **Press feedback:** buttons translate into their shadow on `:active` (`translate(3px,3px)` + shadow shrinks). No opacity hovers.
- **Corners:** small radius (`--radius: 0.375rem`). Never fully rounded pills; never sharp-cornered inputs.
- **Color:** flat blocks only. Big saturated primary areas are encouraged (badges, hero, share cards).
- **Type:** chunky — headings `font-bold`/`font-semibold`, generous tracking-tight on display sizes.

## Gradients (not used)

Neobrutalism is flat by definition — gradients would fight the thick-border/hard-shadow language. None generated. Re-run `brand-design` if this changes.

## Tone and voice

Two registers, split by surface:

**Marketing surfaces (landing, share cards, empty states):** loose, self-aware, internet-native. Lowercase is fine. Specific beats enthusiastic. Example:

> "creators are sitting on 4,200 SOL of unclaimed fees. check yours in 10 seconds."

**Money surfaces (scan results, claim bundles, policy builder):** direct, specific, number-forward. Every amount itemized, every action stated plainly — people sign transactions here, and trust is the product. Example:

> "Claim 2.41 SOL from 3 tokens. Swap 60% to USDC → treasury. FeeSweep fee: 0.07 SOL (3%), itemized below."

### Words to avoid

"Agent", "AI", "bot" — never user-facing (product principle #2). Hype words (revolutionary, unleash, supercharge). "Simply"/"just"/"easy". Corporate verbs (leverage, utilize). Never manufacture urgency on money surfaces.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use shadcn CSS variables (`bg-background`, `text-foreground`, `bg-primary`) everywhere. Never hardcode hex.
- Keep the 4px spacing grid (`gap-2`, `p-4`, `px-6`).
- Use `font-mono tabular-nums` for any number that updates in place (balances, accrual counters).
- Border + hard shadow travel together: a brutal card is `border-[3px] border-foreground shadow-[var(--shadow-brutal-lg)]`.
- Test every component in both light and dark mode. Dark mode is the degen default; light is the share-card default.

**Don't:**

- Hardcode colors in component files.
- Use `transition: all` — specify `transition-colors`, `transition-transform`.
- Use blurred/soft shadows anywhere (`shadow-md`, `shadow-lg` are banned — brutal shadows only).
- Use `rounded-full` on containers (avatars/dots exempt).
- Override tokens per-component — fix it once in `globals.css`.
